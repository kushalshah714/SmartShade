The Problem

Your patio needs shade. But there is nothing to attach it to.


Most motorized shade systems work by running fabric between two side tracks one fixed to each wall. Pull the shade down and the tracks hold everything firmly in place. Clean, simple, effective.

But what if your space has no walls on both sides? Open patios, freestanding pergolas, columns these spaces have nothing to anchor a track to. Most contractors will tell you it cannot be done. SmartShade was built for exactly that situation.

SmartShade works in spaces like these

  • ✓Open patios with no surrounding walls on the sides
  • ✓Pergolas supported by columns or posts rather than solid walls
  • ✓Wide or unusually shaped openings that tracks cannot span
  • ✓Any outdoor space where you want shade without adding permanent structure
How It Works

Instead of tracks on two walls, SmartShade uses two thin steel cables.


Picture a curtain rod but running vertically, one on each side of your opening. The shade fabric travels down these cables as it deploys, staying straight and taut without needing a wall on either side to hold it.

At the top sits a compact motorized roller. Press a button and the shade drops smoothly. Press it again and it rolls back up and disappears. No bulky framing. No structural changes to your home.

How SmartShade Deploys
01

Two Cables, Not Two Walls

A thin stainless steel cable runs down each side of your opening, anchored at the top and bottom. The shade fabric glides along these cables as it extends. No wall required, no track, no channel, no framing to build.

02

A Roller That Disappears

When the shade is up, almost nothing is visible. The fabric wraps around a slim roller at the top of the opening with no bulky casing around it. Neighbours will not notice it. You barely will either.

03

One Button Does It All

A motor inside the roller does the work. Use the included remote, a wall switch, or connect it to your smart home app. Drop the shade before you sit outside. Raise it when you are done.

Why Exterior Shade Works

Stop heat before it gets inside. Interior blinds cannot do that.

Interior blinds slow light down after it has already passed through the glass and turned into heat inside your room. SmartShade sits on the outside, blocking solar energy at the source. The moment it drops, you feel the difference.

Cooler Indoors

Solar heat stopped at the glass means your rooms stay cooler on hot afternoons and your air conditioning has far less work to do.

Glare Gone

Sun on screens, squinting at lunch, afternoon light through west-facing glass. SmartShade handles all of it while keeping the space feeling open.

Fresh Air Stays

You still get the breeze. You still see the garden. Shade without turning your outdoor room into an enclosed box.

Is It Right for You?

A strong fit for some spaces. The wrong call for others.


We would rather tell you upfront if a different Wizard product would serve you better. Here is a straight read on where SmartShade is the right answer and where it is not.

  • ✓Your patio or pergola has no solid walls on the sides
  • ✓Your structure is supported by columns or posts, not walls
  • ✓You have a wide or irregular opening that tracks cannot bridge
  • ✓Your main goal is sun and heat control, not insect protection
  • ✓You want shade without any major structural work
  • ✕Your main goal is keeping insects out
If insects are the priority: a track-guided screen system creates a sealed edge that keeps insects out. SmartShade does not. Wizard Screens & Shades offers motorized track systems for exactly that purpose.
